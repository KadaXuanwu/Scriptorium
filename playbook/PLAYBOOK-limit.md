# Playbook: a model's limit

A part of `PLAYBOOK.md`, in its folder. Read it when your session goes on with another model because the account
reached the limit of the model you ran on. A name in brackets is a part of `PLAYBOOK.md`.

**A model's limit.** A plan can limit one model apart from the rest. If your account reaches that limit while you run
on the model, the script resumes your session on another model, and its message says what to do. If the decisions the
ticket is on the first model for are made and written down, finish on the other model and name both models in the PR
body. If not, commit and push, post one handoff comment (done, the decisions still open, next), write `handoff.md` in
the queue folder, and end without a PR. A worker whose account has the first model left then starts the ticket fresh
from your comment.
